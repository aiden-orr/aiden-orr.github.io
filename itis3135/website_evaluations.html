Website Evaluations

Apple

Apple is an example of a well-designed website because it uses a clean layout, consistent styling, strong visual hierarchy, and effective spacing.

  • Contrast: Apple uses strong contrast between text, backgrounds, images, and buttons. Large product names and headlines stand out clearly from smaller supporting text.
  • Repetition: The website consistently repeats the same fonts, button styles, navigation structure, colors, and product presentation throughout its pages.
  • Alignment: Text, images, buttons, and product sections are carefully aligned. The consistent layout makes the page feel organized and easy to scan.
  • Proximity: Related information is grouped together. Product names, descriptions, prices, and buttons appear near the product they describe, while separate products have enough space between them.

Gates N Fences

Gates N Fences is an example of a poorly designed website because it has a dated appearance, crowded content, and weak visual organization.

  • Contrast: The website uses many different colors, text styles, and images, but the contrast does not create a clear visual hierarchy. It can be difficult to tell what information is most important.
  • Repetition: Some design elements are repeated, but the overall styling is inconsistent. Different fonts, colors, image sizes, and content layouts make the site feel less unified.
  • Alignment: Much of the content does not follow a clean or consistent alignment. Text, images, links, and product information are placed in ways that make the page feel cluttered.
  • Proximity: Large amounts of information are placed close together with limited whitespace. This makes it harder to separate related sections and quickly scan the page.
